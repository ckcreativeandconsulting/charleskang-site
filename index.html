A brief biography

I'm a product leader and hands-on builder with more than a decade of experience across financial services, enterprise platforms, AI products, SaaS, and mobile apps.

At JPMorgan Wealth Management, I led product work for advisor-facing technology, including the Wealth pilot of an internal advisor research chatbot on a broader platform serving thousands of users. My work has often sat between users, engineering, data science, design, business stakeholders, risk, and compliance.

I also build independent software through CK Creative and Consulting LLC, including privacy-focused consumer apps and lightweight tools for everyday workflows.
